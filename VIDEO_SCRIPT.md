# Nook — 3-Minute Demo Video Script

**Total runtime: 3 minutes 00 seconds**
Record with `WATSONX_USE_MOCK=false` and real credentials so the AI generation is visible.
Use a screen recorder (OBS, Loom, Camtasia). Keep your voice calm, deliberate, confident.

---

## SEGMENT 1 — The Problem (0:00 – 0:30)

**[Screen: a blank page, or just your face on camera — no need for the app yet]**

> "Most small business owners know what they want to say.
> They know their product, they know their customers.
> But sitting down to write a Facebook post, an email, a LinkedIn update,
> and a blog intro — all consistent, all on-brand — that's where things fall apart.
>
> Existing AI tools give you one text box, one result.
> If it's wrong, you start again.
> Nothing remembers your brand voice.
> Nothing understands your goal.
> And nothing gives you a full, ready-to-use campaign across every platform at once.
>
> I built Nook to fix that."

**[Cut to browser — show the Nook welcome screen, hero visible]**

---

## SEGMENT 2 — The Product First Impression (0:30 – 0:45)

**[Screen: Nook welcome screen — dark hero, animated orbs, "Your AI Marketing Studio" headline visible]**

> "This is Nook — an AI marketing studio built for small businesses.
> It's powered by IBM watsonx Granite.
> You answer six plain questions, and it produces a complete campaign —
> posts, email, blog, hashtags, SEO keywords, and image prompts —
> all in under two minutes."

**[Point to the 6 feature tiles visible on screen]**

---

## SEGMENT 3 — Live Demo: The Brief (0:45 – 1:30)

**[Click "Start Building" — chat screen appears]**

> "Let me show you. I'll use a real business as an example — a local bakery called Sunny Bakes."

**[Type answers as you speak — keep it natural, don't rush]**

| Question on screen | Your answer | What to say |
|---|---|---|
| What's your business name? | `Sunny Bakes` | "Business name — Sunny Bakes." |
| What industry? | `Bakery` | "Industry — bakery." |
| Target audience? | `Local families and young professionals` | "Targeting local families and young professionals." |
| Campaign goal? | `Drive foot traffic to our new shop` | "Goal — drive foot traffic to the new shop." |
| Brand tone? | `Warm, friendly, and approachable` | "Tone — warm, friendly, approachable." |
| Primary platform? | `Instagram` | "Primary platform — Instagram." |

> "Six questions. That's it. Now watch."

**[Hit Enter on the last answer — typing indicator appears]**

> "IBM watsonx Granite is generating the campaign right now — a real API call, not a template."

---

## SEGMENT 4 — Live Demo: The Campaign Output (1:30 – 2:20)

**[Campaign dashboard animates in — show the full screen]**

> "And here's the full campaign package."

**[Scroll slowly through each section as you describe it]**

> "At the top — the call to action. One clear message Sunny Bakes can use across everything.
>
> Below that — the platform cards. Facebook post, Instagram caption, LinkedIn copy, email with subject line, and a full blog opener.
> Each one is written for that platform's format and tone.
>
> Down here — five SEO keywords for the website.
> And five hashtags ready to paste directly into Instagram.
>
> Finally — three image prompt descriptions for the designer or for an AI image tool."

**[Click "Copy" on the Instagram card]**

> "Every card has a one-click copy button.
> Or hit 'Copy All' to export the entire campaign as plain text."

**[Show the IBM watsonx badge at the top of the output]**

> "And you can see here — generated by IBM watsonx Granite. The real model, the real API."

---

## SEGMENT 5 — Architecture in 20 Seconds (2:20 – 2:40)

**[Optional: switch to a simple diagram or code editor showing watsonx_client.py briefly]**

> "Under the hood it's straightforward.
> The six answers become a structured prompt.
> That prompt goes to the watsonx.ai REST API — Granite model, 1800 tokens.
> The response comes back as validated JSON with 11 fields.
> Flask parses it. The browser renders it. That's the whole stack.
>
> Python backend, SQLite sessions, vanilla JS frontend — no framework overhead.
> Built entirely with IBM Bob as the development tool."

## Recording Checklist

Before you hit record:

- [ ] `WATSONX_USE_MOCK=false` and credentials set
- [ ] App running at `http://localhost:5000`
- [ ] Browser zoomed to 100% or 110% — text must be readable
- [ ] Screen recorder capturing both browser and microphone
- [ ] Do one dry run at 1.5× speed to check timing
- [ ] Final recording target: **2:50 – 3:00** (leave a 5-second buffer)
